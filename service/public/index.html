How it works

Corporate actions are rare. Most tokens go months without one, so most rows on the board read stable.

The board shows accrued multiplier drift, meaning how far each token’s live multiplier has moved from 1.000000. That is reinvested dividends already settled onchain, and it is the data that exists today.

A scheduled change shows as pending with a countdown. It is not live until its effective time, and it can still be cancelled before then.

Balances never change. The amount a holder sees is the raw balance times the multiplier.

What to call

  • GET /v1/tokensEvery token with its live multiplier and any pending change.
  • GET /v1/actions/upcomingScheduled changes, soonest first.
  • GET /v1/actions/recentAnnouncements, schedules and cancellations.
  • GET /v1/tokens/{address}/historyEvery multiplier change for one token.
  • GET /v1/tokens/{address}/multiplier?at=The multiplier at any unix timestamp.
  • POST /v1/subscriptionsSigned webhooks before and when a change lands.
  • GET /v1/streamServer-sent events as they are indexed.
