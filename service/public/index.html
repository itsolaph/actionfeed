Integrate

Balances never change. What a holder sees is the raw balance times the multiplier, so read the multiplier before you display or settle an amount.

  • GET /v1/tokensEvery token with its live multiplier and any pending change.
  • GET /v1/actions/upcomingScheduled changes, soonest first.
  • GET /v1/actions/recentAnnouncements, schedules and cancellations.
  • GET /v1/tokens/{address}/historyEvery multiplier change for one token.
  • GET /v1/tokens/{address}/multiplier?at=The multiplier at any unix timestamp.
  • POST /v1/subscriptionsSigned webhooks before and when a change lands.
  • GET /v1/streamServer-sent events as they are indexed.
